## What it is

Boat care for owners in Auckland, in four services: Essential Reset (the wash-up and rubbish), Valet (beds and full interior), Provisioning (food, drinks, bait, ice) and Charter Ready (interior and superstructure detail). Hospitality for boat owners, not a cleaning company.

## Who it serves

Owners coming off a day or weekend on the water, tired, usually on a phone on the pontoon or in the driveway. Berth holders on the North Shore and at Pine Harbour Marina, and trailer-boat owners on the North Shore and in Whangaparāoa, Beachlands and Maraetai.

"I can leave the boat as it is, and it will be clean, made up and stocked when I come back."
